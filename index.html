Game Setup Guide

Follow these 3 easy steps to get up and running using Prism Launcher
Step 1

Download Prism Launcher

Prism Launcher is an open-source Minecraft launcher that makes managing custom profiles easy.

  1. Go to the official website and download the installer for your operating system.
  2. Run the installer and complete the setup wizard.
  3. Launch the application once installed and select your preferred language/Java version.
Go to Prism Download Page
Step 2

Sign In to Your Account

When you start the launcher for the first time, log into your Minecraft account.

  1. Click "Sign in with Microsoft" and follow the prompts.
⚠️ Tip: If it errors out, try again in half an hour.
Step 3

Import the Profile
